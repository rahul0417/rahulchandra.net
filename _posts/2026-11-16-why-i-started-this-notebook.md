---
title: Why I started this notebook
topic: Notes
summary: A home for my experiments with AI, sales, marketing and products, written down as I go.
---

I test a lot of things: AI tools, sales approaches, marketing ideas, small product bets. Most of what I learn ends up scattered across notes apps, chats and screenshots, and then it's gone.

This site fixes that. Every time I try something worth remembering, I'll write it up here:

1. **What I tried.** The setup, the tool, the idea.
2. **What happened.** The actual result, good or bad.
3. **What I'd do next.** What I learned and what I'd change.

Topics I expect to cover:

- AI experiments and workflows
- Sales and marketing
- Building products
- Customer behaviour

If something here is useful to you, that's a bonus.
